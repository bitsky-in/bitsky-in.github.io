Privacy Policy

Sahrdaya SSJ Launcher
Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Sahrdaya SSJ Launcher is a simple Android application developed by Sahrdaya College of Engineering and Technology.

1. Information We Collect

Sahrdaya SSJ Launcher does not collect, store, process, or share any personal information from users.

The application does not request or access:

  • Name or contact information
  • Location
  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts
  • Photos or files
  • Device identifiers
  • Financial or payment information
  • Usage analytics or tracking information

2. How the App Works

Sahrdaya SSJ Launcher provides a simple interactive button. When the user taps the button, the application displays an animation.

This interaction is processed locally on the user's device. No user information is transmitted to any server.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any personal information because the application does not collect personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Sahrdaya SSJ Launcher does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the application does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or other users.

6. Data Security

Because the application does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal data is stored on our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of the application changes. Any updated version will be made available at the same location as this Privacy Policy.
